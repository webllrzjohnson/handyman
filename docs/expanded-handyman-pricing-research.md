# Expanded Toronto/GTA Handyman Pricing Research
This file consolidates Research & Content outputs from the door/hardware, screens/windows/mounting, and maintenance research batches. Prices are CAD planning ranges, not guarantees. Classified/Kijiji/Reddit evidence is noisy and should only set an informal floor, not Louie’s default quote.
## Source quality
- Best sources were local/GTA pages with published ranges: TaskRabbit Toronto/Canada guide, BuildMan flat-rate Toronto guide, HomeStars Toronto locksmith/handyman pages, Taskpin Toronto door repair, Ontario Door Repair residential/commercial cost guides, RenoHouse weatherstrip page, Matrix/247 GTA/770 locksmith smart-lock/deadbolt pages, plus Kijiji listings. Evidence is strongest for lock/deadbolt, latch/hinge, weatherstrip, and smart-lock work; weaker for stopper, peephole, and chain/swing-bar because local providers rarely publish fixed line-item pricing. Kijiji mostly confirmed local availability and low-price marketplace positioning, not reliable final quotes. Reddit was used only as weak buyer-sentiment/context, not as GTA pricing proof.
- Moderate for mounting and general handyman rates (TaskRabbit/UrbanTasker/Taskpin/local pages align). Moderate for window screens/cranks (several GTA specialists publish ranges). Weak for screen spline, frame corner, screen-door closer and exact quantity discounts because sources usually bundle them into re-screen/door hardware service calls. Kijiji/Reddit/Facebook buyer sentiment was accessible mostly as snippets and is labelled weak where used.
- Strongest sources: HomeStars Toronto 2026 hourly ranges ($60-$120/hr), TaskRabbit Canada/Toronto average ($39/hr, $91 invoice), BuildMan GTA flat minimum ($150 first hour), Toronto Fire smoke-alarm guidance, ESA licensed-electrical rules, Ontario fuel-certificate regulation, and local HVAC/electrical pages for company comparisons. Weak evidence: Kijiji search snippets and SEO/local blog pricing; use only as low-end market signal. Exact battery/filter-only prices are rarely published, so those rows are inferred from observed Toronto minimum charges, task time, and company service-call comparisons.
