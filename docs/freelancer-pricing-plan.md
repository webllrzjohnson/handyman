# Freelancer Pricing Research Plan

## Purpose

The app should not rely only on established company pricing. It should compare three layers of the Toronto market:

1. Informal freelancer pricing from Kijiji, Reddit, Facebook Marketplace where accessible, Craigslist, TaskRabbit, Airtasker, and local classifieds.
2. Small business and handyman company pricing.
3. Licensed trade pricing for referral-only work.

## Why this matters

A capable freelancer may need to charge less than a fully insured service company, but still enough to cover time, travel, tools, risk, materials, callbacks, and job uncertainty.

## Pricing modes to add

### Friendly side job
Use for referrals, repeat clients, nearby jobs, low risk, and easy access.

### Standard freelancer
Use for normal public clients, one-off jobs, moderate travel, and basic materials.

### Professional service
Use for urgent jobs, higher-risk jobs, difficult access, condo delays, parking, and jobs where callback risk is higher.

## Formula to use

Final quote equals:

- Labour base
- Materials or consumables
- Travel/transport allowance
- Parking or transit cost
- Access difficulty
- Urgency premium
- Condition adjustment
- Risk/callback allowance
- Minimum visit floor

## Expense fields to add

- Travel distance: nearby, normal, far
- Transportation: driving, transit, walking/local
- Parking: none, estimated, actual
- Access: house, condo/elevator/security, difficult access
- Materials: included, separate, actual cost
- Minimum visit: enabled by default
- Pricing mode: friendly, standard freelancer, professional service

## Research targets

- Kijiji Toronto/GTA handyman ads
- TaskRabbit Toronto handyman, mounting, furniture assembly pages
- Airtasker Ontario/Toronto handyman pages
- Reddit Toronto threads where available
- Craigslist Toronto services, if accessible
- Facebook Marketplace/groups, if publicly accessible
- Local handyman pages with individual or small-operator pricing

## Source quality rules

- Treat official ESA/TSSA/municipal sources as strong for licensing boundaries.
- Treat company pricing as medium quality for market ceiling.
- Treat classifieds as useful but noisy.
- Treat Reddit comments as weak unless multiple comments agree.
- Do not use one unusually cheap post as the recommended price.

## App output goal

For each job, show:

- Freelancer low, fair, and high range
- Company market range
- Suggested price for Louie
- Expenses included in the calculation
- When to charge extra
- When to refer or refuse the job
- Client-ready message

## Current early findings

Initial search found Kijiji ads as low as $50/hour, TaskRabbit pages showing projects starting around $69 and furniture assembly hourly rates starting around $34, plus other GTA guides showing $75 to $125/hour as more normal handyman pricing. These are early findings and need cleanup before importing into app data.
